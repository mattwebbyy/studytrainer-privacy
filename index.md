# StudyTrainer privacy policy

**Last updated: 25 October 2026**

## What we collect

You can use StudyTrainer without an account. If you create one, we collect your
email address and your study progress so you can sign in and sync between
devices. If you turn on usage statistics, we also collect basic information
about how the app is used. Like most online services, we receive basic
technical information, such as your IP address, when you use online features.

## How we use it

Only to provide the app: signing you in, syncing your progress, sending account
emails, and improving StudyTrainer. We do not sell your data or use it for
advertising.

## Your choices

You can turn off usage statistics, export your data, or delete your account at
any time in Settings.

## Children

StudyTrainer is not directed at children under 13.
